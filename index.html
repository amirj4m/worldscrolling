FAQ

Quick questions.

Is this a historical tour?

Nope. The Acropolis is great and you'll find it without us. worldscrolling is about the places locals actually hang out: food, bars, viewpoints and hidden gems.

Is it a subscription?

No. Every package is a one-off payment per trip.

How does the WhatsApp help work?

You get a direct WhatsApp line to your local. Ask anything — where to eat right now, how to get somewhere, what to do tonight. On Local Friend on Call and above, it's a true 24/7 concierge during your trip.

When do I get my plan?

Before your trip. Tell us your dates and vibe, and your personalised plan and map land in your inbox and WhatsApp ahead of arrival.
